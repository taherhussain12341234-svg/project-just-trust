The Lost Moon Buggy

In 1971, Apollo 15 astronauts drove the first car on the Moon. When it was time to go home, they parked it near Hadley Rille. It is still there today.

8 mphTop speed
17.3 miTotal distance driven
462 lbWeight on Earth
Why leave it? Every pound left behind made room for more Moon rocks. The crew brought home about 170 pounds of them.
